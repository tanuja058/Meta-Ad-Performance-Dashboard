★ Meta Ads Performance Dashboard

Facebook & Instagram Advertising Analytics using Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze and monitor the performance of **Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns**. The project focuses on transforming advertising data into actionable business insights through KPIs, interactive filters, and visual analytics.

---

★ Business Problem

Digital advertising teams generate large amounts of campaign data across platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. However, raw campaign data alone does not clearly show which campaigns, audiences, platforms, or advertising activities are contributing to better performance.

The objective of this project is to build an interactive dashboard that helps marketing teams:

* Monitor overall advertising performance
* Compare Facebook and Instagram performance
* Analyze campaign-level performance
* Track key advertising KPIs
* Identify high-performing and underperforming campaigns
* Understand audience and engagement patterns
* Support data-driven marketing decisions

---

★ Project Objective

The main objective is to convert advertising campaign data into a **clear and interactive business intelligence dashboard** that enables users to quickly identify performance trends and opportunities for optimization.

 ★ Key questions addressed

1. How are Facebook and Instagram campaigns performing?
2. Which platform generates better campaign performance?
3. Which campaigns contribute the most to overall results?
4. Which campaigns require optimization?
5. How does advertising spend relate to campaign performance?
6. Which KPIs should marketers monitor regularly?
7. What trends can be identified from campaign performance data?

★ DAX & Analytical Measures

The project uses calculated measures to evaluate advertising performance.

Examples include:

```DAX
Total Spend = SUM(Data[Spend])
```

```DAX
Total Clicks = SUM(Data[Clicks])
```

★ Business Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify insights such as:

★ Platform Performance

Compare Facebook and Instagram to understand differences in advertising performance and engagement.

★ Campaign Performance

Identify campaigns that generate stronger results and campaigns that may require optimization.

★ Advertising Efficiency

Analyze the relationship between advertising expenditure and campaign outcomes.

★ KPI Monitoring

Monitor CTR, CPC, impressions, clicks, engagement, and other relevant metrics to evaluate campaign efficiency.

★Performance Trends

Use interactive filtering to explore changes in campaign performance across available dimensions.


★ Business Recommendations

Based on the patterns identified through the dashboard, marketing teams can:

* Allocate more attention to consistently high-performing campaigns.
* Review campaigns with high spending but comparatively weak results.
* Monitor CTR and CPC to identify advertising efficiency issues.
* Compare Facebook and Instagram performance before allocating future budgets.
* Use campaign-level analysis to improve targeting and creative strategies.
* Continuously monitor KPIs instead of relying only on total campaign results.
